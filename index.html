Calculation method and quality checks

Test 7H step (5) permits three circles to overlap, or to have no common intersection. This worksheet reports a numerical best-fit plot and discrepancy, rather than falsely treating an arbitrary residual threshold as an AMM prohibition. The numerical least-squares fit has NOT been validated as equivalent to the graphical constructions in step 5(a)/(b). Do not use numerical output as installation authority.
